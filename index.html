Operating Philosophy

The 7-rule delivery system I install inside agencies.

Most agency margin is not lost in code - it leaks in vague estimates, verbal approvals, and untracked revisions. These are the exact rules I built across Efir Media, DesigningIT, Verixity, and DigiWay.

Never quote fixed development cost off a sales deck. Separate discovery and UX/UI sign-off into Phase 1, then re-estimate engineering off approved specifications and real edge cases.

Zero work starts on verbal promises. Written revision caps and deposit milestones protect both the agency's cash flow and the client's launch timeline from endless subjective tweaking.

Every rework request that enters development unapproved destroys sprint velocity. A lightweight written stage sign-off turns emotional disputes into calm, documented scope decisions.

Saying "yes" to a new client idea is easy when you pair it with clear impact on timeline and budget. Written change orders turn scope creep from an agency loss into predictable revenue.

Decisions buried in Slack threads disappear when teams rotate or PMs take over cold. One living spec document (plus clean CRM-to-tracker pipelines) eliminates repeated discovery and onboarding chaos.

From 15-minute ERP sync validation to multi-salon rollout guides, a real launch includes end-to-end acceptance checklists so clients can operate confidently after handover.

In my daily practice, AI helps draft structured briefs, edge-case lists, QA checklists, and rapid prototypes faster - while humans stay fully accountable for scope, architecture, and client trust.
